E-KART Platform

A mobile management ecosystem providing real-time store inventory indexing, price comparison routing, and dynamic contextual recommendations.

TELEMETRY AT A GLANCE

  • Cross-platform Python & Kivy application engine

System Overview

E-KART was developed to streamline retail logistics and customer decision-making. By consolidating inventory tracking, price comparison, and navigation into a unified interface, it bridges the gap between store managers and in-aisle shoppers.

Engineering Challenges Overcome

  • Designing a responsive layout in Kivy that scales cleanly across multiple tablet and phone screen ratios.
  • Optimizing database query performance when searching across hundreds of localized SKU records.
  • Structuring clean separation between the business logic layer and the declarative UI components.
