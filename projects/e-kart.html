E-KART

A mobile app concept that brought inventory, recommendations, and nearby-store comparisons into one simple experience.

Open source ↗ Back to projects

At a glance

  • Mobile app interface
  • Inventory-oriented workflow
  • Simple recommendation concept

Overview

E-KART was built as a practical mobile app prototype for store management and decision support. The aim was to bring multiple pieces of store information into one place without making the experience feel too crowded.

Problem

Store management often means bouncing between several tasks and views. A single app can help make that easier when it is organized around the real flow of the work.

My Approach

I focused on keeping the app practical and readable. The interaction model was shaped around a simple idea: make useful information accessible without overloading the screen.

Technologies Used

  • Python
  • Kivy
  • Mobile UI work

Challenges

  • Keeping the interface clear while supporting multiple features
  • Designing interactions that still felt natural on a small screen
  • Balancing scope with the time available for the prototype

What I Learned

  • How interface choices affect how useful an app feels in practice
  • How a simple app can still support complex user needs
  • How to keep a prototype moving without losing focus on the core experience

Future Improvements

  • Refine navigation so the app flows more naturally
  • Improve data handling for real-store use cases
  • Explore a more complete version with clearer product feedback
